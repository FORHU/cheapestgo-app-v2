# Translations awaiting native review

Strings v2 says that v1 never did, so there was no human translation to reuse. The Korean,
Japanese and Chinese below were written by Claude and **have not been checked by a native
speaker**. They are live — a reader sees them today — so this is a list of what to check,
not of what is missing.

Everything not listed here came from v1 and was translated by a person.

Checkout wording carries weight: it is where someone hands over money, and a phrase that
reads oddly there costs more than one on a marketing page. Review these first.
